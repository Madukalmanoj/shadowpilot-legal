# Privacy Policy for ShadowPilot

**Effective Date: September 6, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how ShadowPilot ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information.

---

## 1. Information Collected

### A. Google User Data (OAuth 2.0)
When you sign in using Google Auth, we access only the following non-sensitive information:
- **Email Address**: To authenticate your account and link your subscription tier.
- **Basic Profile Info** (Name, Avatar URL): To display your profile in the application.

We do **not** access your Google Drive, Gmail messages, Google Contacts, Google Calendar, or any sensitive Google user data.

### B. Desktop Audio and Screen Context
- Any audio or visual context captured while using the assistant is processed in real-time.
- We do not store, archive, or sell your audio recordings or screen snapshots.

---

## 2. Use of Information
Your information is used strictly to:
- Authenticate and manage your account session.
- Prevent fraudulent usage and enforce free/pro quotas.
- Provide direct customer support upon request.

---

## 3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)
ShadowPilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the **Google API Services User Data Policy**, including the Limited Use requirements:
- We do not use Google user data to serve advertisements.
- We do not allow human review of your Google data without your explicit consent for support purposes.
- We do not sell or transfer your data to any third party.

---

## 4. User Data Retention & Deletion
You may request the deletion of your account and all associated records at any time by emailing **shadowpilot.app@gmail.com**. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
